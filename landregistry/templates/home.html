Who it is for

Three desks, one register.

01

Landowners

Open your own account and add your land. After an official approves it, the UPI and the title appear on your record.

02

Authorized land officials

Approve or reject land a landowner has added. Approval issues the UPI and the title and seals the parcel.

03

Administrators

Issue official accounts, follow the audit log, and prepare land management reports. Landowners register themselves. The administrator password stays admin.

How a record moves

From a visit to a sealed block.

  1. AccountA landowner registers with a real name, email, and Rwandan phone number.
  2. RegistrationA landowner submits the place, size, and use. An official approves it, and the system then issues the UPI and the title.
  3. SealThe entry is hashed with the previous block, so a quiet edit breaks the chain.
  4. TitleThe landowner opens the parcel and sees the issued UPI and title. The record can be printed or downloaded.
